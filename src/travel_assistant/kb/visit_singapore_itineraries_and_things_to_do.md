Sample itinerary patterns for Singapore usually mix iconic landmarks, food exploration, neighborhood culture, and optional family-focused experiences.

Three-day planning pattern:
Day 1 (city icons):
- Marina Bay landmarks and waterfront walk.
- Gardens by the Bay (outdoor gardens plus indoor conservatories as rain-safe alternatives).
- Evening light and skyline viewpoints.

Day 2 (culture and heritage):
- Chinatown heritage walk and temple visit.
- Little India or Kampong Glam cultural exploration.
- Hawker-focused local food trail.

Day 3 (family or leisure):
- Sentosa attractions or nature-focused parks.
- Indoor substitutions such as museums, aquariums, or science centres when rain is expected.
- Shopping and neighborhood cafe time for flexible pacing.

Indoor / outdoor substitution guidance:
- Outdoor candidates: gardens, waterfront routes, neighborhood walking tours, parks.
- Indoor alternatives: museums, conservatories, indoor observation areas, aquariums, and curated exhibits.

Transport planning note:
- MRT-led routing is effective for day plans that span multiple districts.
- Same-district clustering is recommended for half-day activity blocks.
