Singapore is compact, safe, and easy to navigate for first-time visitors. Commonly visited areas include Marina Bay, Chinatown, Little India, Kampong Glam, Orchard Road, and Sentosa.

Attractions and neighborhoods:
- Marina Bay: skyline viewpoints, Merlion area, Gardens by the Bay, and waterfront walks.
- Chinatown: heritage shophouses, temples, local food centers, and cultural streets.
- Little India: colorful streets, Indian heritage spots, and vegetarian dining options.
- Kampong Glam: Malay-Arab heritage, Sultan Mosque area, boutique cafes, and murals.
- Sentosa: family-friendly attractions, beaches, and indoor entertainment options.

Transportation guidance:
- MRT is typically the fastest and most reliable way to move between districts.
- EZ-Link or contactless bank cards are commonly used for public transport fares.
- Buses cover routes not directly served by MRT and are useful for local access.
- Taxis and ride-hailing are available when weather or time constraints matter.

Practical travel tips:
- Indoor options are useful during rain or midday heat.
- Carry water and lightweight clothing; many attractions involve walking.
- Hawker centres are cost-effective for local food experiences.
- Queueing etiquette and cleanliness rules are strongly observed.

Food and local experiences:
- Hawker culture is a key local experience with multi-cuisine options.
- Popular food areas include Lau Pa Sat, Maxwell Food Centre, and neighborhood hawker centres.
- Travelers often pair food stops with nearby cultural districts for efficient itineraries.
